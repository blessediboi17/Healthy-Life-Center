Privacy Policy

Healthy Life Center

This page explains what information Healthy Life Center collects when you use this website, and how it's used.

What we collect

Browsing our product catalog doesn't require an account or any personal details. We only collect information you choose to give us, such as:

  • Your name and contact details, if you submit them through the customer service form on this site
  • The contents of your cart and any order details you send us when you check out via WhatsApp
  • Basic, anonymous page-visit counts, used only to understand how many people are viewing the site

How we use it

We use the information you share to respond to your order, answer questions, arrange delivery, and follow up on any complaint or request submitted through the customer service form. We don't sell or share your information with third parties for advertising.

Orders and WhatsApp

When you check out, your order details are sent to us directly through WhatsApp. That conversation is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy, since it's their platform carrying the message, not ours.

Data storage

Product and inquiry information is stored securely using Supabase, our database provider. We keep customer service submissions only as long as needed to resolve them.

Your choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, or to request it be deleted, using the contact details below.
